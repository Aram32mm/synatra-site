A few common questions

How do I sign in?

Use the sign-in method offered by your installed version and the account you used to join. Some test builds use an invited email address. If sign-in is refused, email support with the message you see.

[OWNER: confirm] The release sign-in flow. The server supports Sign in with Apple, but the reviewed iPhone build still has an email-only sign-in screen.

Why does the first week take longer?

New exercises start with a calibration ramp to find a suitable working weight. Allow some extra time for those early sessions. By week two, familiar exercises have your logged sets to work from; a new exercise still needs its own ramp.

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

Yes. Open Settings → Preferences → Units, choose Kilograms or Pounds, and save. Your gym’s bar, plates and dumbbells can be set separately so suggested loads match the equipment you use.

My Apple Watch isn’t connecting. What should I try?

Make sure the Watch is paired with your iPhone and Synatra is installed on both devices. Open Synatra on your iPhone, sign in, then open it on your Watch with the devices nearby. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled.

If it still does not connect, open Settings → Watch link on the iPhone and try “Push account to watch now”. If that fails, share the connection status with support. Do not send a sign-in token.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open Synatra on your iPhone and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete account and select it.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

Successful deletion removes your account and training records from the active service and signs you out. There is no undo. Original Apple Health records remain in Health. See the privacy policy for backups and the minimal deletion record.

[OWNER: confirm] Apple-linked accounts need a fresh Apple authorization step that is not yet wired into the reviewed iPhone build. If this affects you, contact support; do not assume an error means your account was deleted.
