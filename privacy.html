Who is responsible

What we collect and why

Account and profile
Your email, name, and linked Apple account identifier where applicable; sex, age, body weight, optional height, time zone, training experience and goals, equipment, availability and training preferences. Account details identify your account. Training settings and profile information help plan sessions and estimate starting loads. Height is optional and is not used by the training engine.
Workouts and sets
Exercises, loads, reps, reported and prescribed reps in reserve, session times, edits and the plan behind a session. These record your training, guide progression and calculate fatigue and recovery.
Daily check-ins
Information you choose to report, including sleep, soreness, illness, stress, nutrition, sleep quality, alcohol and joint discomfort. Readiness currently uses logged sets, elapsed time, sleep duration, illness and soreness. Other reported signals are recorded as history; they do not currently change readiness.
Apple Health, with your permission
The iPhone app requests read access to sleep, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability (HRV) and body weight. Imported readings are sent to Synatra’s server. Sleep duration informs readiness. HRV, resting heart rate and body-weight observations are recorded but are not current readiness inputs. This is separate from the profile weight used to estimate starting loads. During a Watch session, the app reads heart rate and can save the workout to Apple Health with permission.
Account security and operation
The server stores sign-in session records, including a hash of the sign-in token, issuance and use times, expiry and revocation. Operational logs support running and troubleshooting the service. [OWNER: confirm] The deployed logs’ exact fields, providers and retention period.

Where your data is stored

The backend is configured to run on Fly.io in San Jose, California, United States (sjc). The database uses Neon. Encrypted database backups are stored as GitHub Actions artifacts. These providers process data to operate Synatra.

[OWNER: confirm] The Neon database region, any additional service-provider processing locations, and that deployed infrastructure matches these repository settings.

Retention and backups

The code keeps account and training records until account deletion; no general automatic expiry for inactive accounts was found. [OWNER: confirm] The intended retention policy for inactive accounts and support correspondence.

A scheduled nightly job creates an encrypted database backup. Its GitHub artifact retention is set to 90 days. A deleted account’s data can remain in older backups until those copies expire. [OWNER: confirm] Actual backup operation, Neon’s own recovery retention and how deletions made after a backup are reapplied after restoring it.

Deleting your account

In the iPhone app, open Settings → Delete account and confirm. On successful deletion, the server removes your profile, workouts, check-ins, observations, plans and sign-in sessions; the app clears its local account data and signals the Watch to clear its copy.

For accounts linked to Apple, the server requires fresh Apple authorization and revokes Sign in with Apple before erasing the account. If Apple authorization fails, nothing is deleted and you can retry.

[OWNER: confirm] The reviewed iPhone build cannot yet request that fresh Apple authorization. Confirm the release includes this flow before publishing it as generally available. If deletion fails, contact support@synatra.watch.

The server retains a minimal deletion record: an internal account ID and the deletion time. This prevents later writes from recreating the account. The documented policy retains this minimal record permanently. Deleting Synatra’s copy does not delete the original records in Apple Health.

This website uses no cookies, analytics, ads, third-party trackers, external scripts or external fonts. No analytics or advertising SDK was found in the reviewed app source.

Your choices and questions

You control Apple Health permissions in your device’s Health settings. You can choose which check-in information to provide. To ask about access, corrections, deletion or this policy, email support@synatra.watch.
